## 🎮 What Is Arena Stick Brawl?

Arena Stick Brawl brings the fun of stickman combat into a browser-based gaming experience.

The concept is simple: enter the arena, learn the controls, master your moves, and fight your way toward victory.

Whether you're here to test your reflexes, enjoy some casual action, or prove that your stickman deserves a championship belt, the arena is waiting.

**Your stickman might be made of lines. Your fighting spirit shouldn't be.**
